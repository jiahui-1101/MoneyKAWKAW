03 · THE QUESTIONS
Every committee asks the same five questions

And today, nobody can answer them quickly.

1How much of each department's budget is used?Spending is only known after receipts are collected, so overruns are found late.
2Which purchases still have no claim?Planned purchases and submitted claims live in different places.
3Is this receipt total correct?Totals are typed by hand, and edits leave no history.
4Has this claim been paid?"Approved" and "paid" get mixed up, with no proof of transfer.
5Did the member actually receive the money?No confirmation step, so a dispute has no record.
04 / 16
04 · THE SOLUTION
MoneyKAWKAW

One linked record, from budget to bank-in.

01BudgetDirector sets the activity and department allocations
02TaskHOD assigns a purchase with an item budget
03ClaimMember snaps the receipt and reviews the OCR
04ReviewTreasurer approves or declines with a reason
05PaymentTransfer recorded with proof
06ConfirmMember confirms receipt or reports a problem

Every dashboard total is derived from these linked records, never typed in by hand.

11 · TRUST BY DESIGN
Hiding a button is not security

Rules enforced by the server, not the screen.

⛨

Scoped access

Postgres row-level security and checked RPCs. A member from one department cannot open another's claims by editing a URL.

••

Masked payout details

Bank and Touch 'n Go details are hidden everywhere except the treasurer's payment step.

↺

Append-only history

Original receipt and OCR kept. Corrections and exclusions stored with before, after, actor and time.

Σ

Derived totals

Amounts in integer cents, computed from linked records. The browser never supplies a trusted total.
